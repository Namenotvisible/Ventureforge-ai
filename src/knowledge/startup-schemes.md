# Indian Government Startup Schemes and Funding

## Startup India Initiative (DPIIT)
- **Programme**: Startup India, launched January 2016 by Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).
- **Eligibility**: Entity must be incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Registered Partnership. Age ≤ 10 years from incorporation. Annual turnover < ₹100 crore in any previous financial year. Working towards innovation, development, or improvement of products/services.
- **Benefits**:
  - Income tax exemption for 3 consecutive years out of the first 10 years.
  - Capital gains tax exemption on investments.
  - Self-certification under 9 labour and 3 environment laws.
  - Fast-track patent examination at 80% rebate on patent fees.
  - Access to government tenders without prior experience requirement.
  - Access to Fund of Funds (FFS) managed by SIDBI.
- **How to Apply**: Register at startupindia.gov.in; upload incorporation documents, business description, and innovation proof.
- Source: DPIIT Startup India Scheme Document, Ministry of Commerce, 2024.

## Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) — SIDBI
- **Programme**: Fund of Funds for Startups managed by Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI).
- **Corpus**: ₹10,000 crore committed by Government of India.
- **How it works**: FFS does not invest directly into startups. It invests into SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) that in turn invest into DPIIT-recognised startups.
- **Eligibility**: Startup must be DPIIT-recognised. The VC/AIF fund managing the investment must be registered with SEBI and approved by SIDBI.
- **Disbursement**: As of March 2024, FFS has committed to 111 AIFs, which have invested in 1,124+ startups.
- Source: SIDBI Annual Report 2023–24, DPIIT FFS Progress Report 2024.

## MUDRA Loans (Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency)
- **Programme**: Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY).
- **Purpose**: Collateral-free loans for micro and small enterprises including early-stage startups.
- **Loan Categories**:
  - Shishu: up to ₹50,000 (early stage, no business history required).
  - Kishore: ₹50,001 – ₹5 lakh.
  - Tarun: ₹5 lakh – ₹10 lakh.
- **Eligibility**: Non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises; manufacturing, trading, or services sector.
- **How to Apply**: Apply through any public sector bank, regional rural bank, microfinance institution, or via udyamimitra.in portal.
- Source: MUDRA Annual Report 2023–24, Ministry of Finance.

## SIDBI Startup Mitra and Direct Lending
- **Programme**: SIDBI Startup Mitra — direct lending to startups.
- **Loan Amount**: ₹10 lakh – ₹5 crore.
- **Interest Rate**: 8.5–12% per annum (as of 2024; linked to repo rate).
- **Tenure**: Up to 5 years.
- **Eligibility**: DPIIT-recognised startup; must have minimum 1 year of operations.
- **Collateral**: Asset-light or collateral-free for loans up to ₹25 lakh with personal guarantee.
- Source: SIDBI Annual Report 2023–24.

## Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) — NITI Aayog
- **Programme**: Atal Innovation Mission promotes innovation and entrepreneurship across India.
- **Schemes under AIM**:
  - **Atal Incubation Centres (AIC)**: Grant of up to ₹10 crore to establish incubation centres in universities and industry.
  - **Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL)**: ₹20 lakh grant per school for innovation labs targeting students.
  - **Atal New India Challenges**: Up to ₹1 crore for deep tech startups solving national challenges.
- **Eligibility**: AIC — established educational/research institutions. Challenges — any DPIIT-recognised startup with working prototype.
- Source: NITI Aayog Atal Innovation Mission Annual Report 2023.

## NASSCOM 10,000 Startups Programme
- **Programme**: Industry body support programme, no equity taken.
- **Benefits**: Mentorship, market access, co-working space, cloud credits (AWS, Google, Microsoft), investor connects.
- **Eligibility**: Indian tech startups under 5 years old, not yet raised Series B.
- Source: NASSCOM Startup Ecosystem Report 2024.

## State-Level Schemes (Selected)
- **T-Hub (Telangana)**: Incubation, investor access, international market connect. No equity.
- **Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM)**: Seed funding up to ₹15 lakh, incubation, mentorship.
- **iStart Rajasthan**: Seed funding up to ₹25 lakh, cloud credits, office space.
- **Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSInS)**: Grants of ₹5–25 lakh for innovative startups.
- Source: Respective state government startup portals, 2024.

## Sector-Specific Schemes
- **PM-KISAN and AGRI funds** (Agritech): Ministry of Agriculture provides innovation grants for agritech.
- **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (Healthtech)**: NHA offers sandbox programs and pilot contracts for digital health startups.
- **National Education Policy (Edtech)**: DIKSHA platform access, potential government pilot contracts.
- **Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme**: For manufacturing and deep tech hardware startups.
- Source: Ministry of Agriculture, NHA, Ministry of Education, 2024.
