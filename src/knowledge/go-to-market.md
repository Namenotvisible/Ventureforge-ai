# Go-To-Market Strategies

## What Is a Go-To-Market Strategy?
A go-to-market (GTM) strategy is a plan for launching a product and reaching target customers. It covers: target segment, value proposition, pricing, channels, and sales motion.

## The Four GTM Stages for Indian Startups

### Stage 1 — Validate (Months 1–3)
Goal: Confirm that the problem is real and your solution creates value.
- Run 20–30 customer discovery interviews.
- Build a landing page or mockup; measure sign-ups or waitlist joins.
- Identify 5–10 design partners willing to use a rough MVP.
- Key metric: Are customers willing to pay or invest time in the solution?

### Stage 2 — Pilot (Months 3–9)
Goal: Build and deploy a narrow MVP with design partners in a single geography or segment.
- Limit to one city or one customer type.
- Instrument usage analytics (user flows, drop-off points).
- Weekly calls with pilot users.
- Key metrics: Retention at 30 days (>40% is strong for consumer, >70% for B2B), NPS.

### Stage 3 — Launch (Months 9–18)
Goal: Achieve repeatable, scalable customer acquisition.
- Open product to wider audience.
- Activate 2–3 acquisition channels (content marketing, community, partnerships, direct sales, paid digital).
- Establish onboarding playbook.
- Key metrics: CAC by channel, CAC payback period, MoM MRR growth (>15% is strong).

### Stage 4 — Scale (Month 18+)
Goal: Grow faster than the market by doubling down on proven channels.
- Hire channel-specific growth owners.
- Expand to new geographies or customer segments.
- Invest in brand and community for organic growth.
- Key metric: LTV:CAC > 3, Payback period < 18 months.

## Customer Acquisition Channels in India

### Digital / Paid
- Google Search Ads: high intent, high cost in competitive categories.
- Meta (Instagram + Facebook): strong for B2C consumer apps, regional language targeting.
- LinkedIn Ads: effective for B2B targeting CTOs, HR managers, founders.
- YouTube Pre-roll: awareness for regional audiences.

### Organic / Content
- SEO and blog content: long-term, compounding; effective for edtech, fintech, health.
- YouTube education channel: very strong in India, especially in regional languages.
- LinkedIn thought leadership: high-quality B2B leads.

### Community and Partnerships
- WhatsApp / Telegram communities: fastest distribution in Tier 2/3 India.
- Regional influencer marketing: micro-influencers at ₹5,000–50,000/post.
- Channel partnerships: resellers, distributors, NGOs (effective for agritech, rural fintech).
- Government tie-ups: common entry points for edtech (DIKSHA), agritech (e-NAM), healthtech.

### Direct Sales (B2B)
- SDR/AE model for enterprise deals above ₹5 lakh/year ACV.
- In-person meetings still required for large Indian enterprises and government clients.

## Startup KPIs by Stage
| Stage | Key Metrics |
|-------|-------------|
| Validate | # interviews, waitlist sign-ups, pilot partners |
| Pilot | 30-day retention, NPS, active users, qualitative feedback |
| Launch | MRR, CAC by channel, conversion rate, churn |
| Scale | MRR growth %, LTV:CAC, payback period, gross margin |

## GTM for Bharat (Tier 2/3 India)
- Vernacular language support is critical: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada.
- Lower price points: ARPU ₹99–499/month vs ₹999+ in metros.
- Offline-first or low-data-use design if targeting rural areas.
- Assisted model: human agents or kiosks for complex products (insurance, credit, health).
- Trust signals matter more: local language, local examples, partner logos.
