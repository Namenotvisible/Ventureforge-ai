# India Startup Funding Landscape

## Overview
India has the world's third-largest startup ecosystem with 100,000+ DPIIT-recognised startups as of 2024. Funding flows from bootstrapping through angels, institutional VCs, and strategic investors.

## Funding Stages and Typical Amounts (India, 2024)

### Bootstrapping / Self-Funded
Founders use personal savings or early revenue. No dilution. Ideal for capital-light models.

### Friends, Family and Fools (FFF)
Informal round before any institutional capital. Typically ₹5–25 lakh.

### Angel / Pre-Seed Funding
- Amount: ₹25 lakh – ₹2 crore.
- Source: Individual angel investors, angel networks (Indian Angel Network, Mumbai Angels, LetsVenture, AngelList India).
- Equity diluted: 10–20%.
- What investors look for: Compelling problem, credible team, early traction (even informal).
- Source: NASSCOM, Indian Angel Network data 2023.

### Seed Funding
- Amount: ₹1 crore – ₹10 crore (roughly $120K–$1.2M).
- Source: Seed funds (Blume Ventures, India Quotient, Antler India, Elevation Micro VC), accelerators (Y Combinator India cohort, Sequoia Surge, 500 Global India).
- What investors look for: MVP built, 10–100 paying customers or strong user traction, clear GTM hypothesis.
- Equity diluted: 15–25%.
- Source: Blume Ventures Indus Valley Report 2024, Tracxn India Funding Report 2023.

### Series A
- Amount: ₹10–80 crore ($1–10M).
- Source: Institutional VCs — Elevation Capital, Nexus Venture Partners, Matrix Partners India, Accel India, Lightspeed India.
- What investors look for: Repeatable CAC, LTV:CAC > 2, MoM revenue growth >10%, clear path to profitability.
- Source: Elevation Capital portfolio data, Accel India portfolio 2023.

### Series B and Beyond
- Amount: ₹80 crore+ ($10M+).
- Source: Tiger Global, SoftBank, Prosus, Steadview Capital, DST Global.
- Key consideration: Focus on unit economics and path to profitability post-2022 funding winter.

## Accelerators and Incubators in India
- **Y Combinator**: Global program, India cohort growing; $500K for 7% equity (2024 standard deal).
- **Sequoia Surge**: India/Southeast Asia seed program. $1–2M for 10–15% equity.
- **Antler India**: Pre-seed/seed; builds teams from scratch.
- **iStart Rajasthan, T-Hub Hyderabad, CIIE.CO (IIM Ahmedabad)**: Government-backed incubators.
- **NASSCOM 10,000 Startups**: No-equity mentorship and market access program.
- Source: NASSCOM Startup Ecosystem Report 2024.

## Valuation Methods at Early Stage
- Pre-revenue: Berkus Method — up to $500K per milestone (team, prototype, strategic relationships, product, sales channels).
- Revenue-stage: Revenue multiple (typically 5–10× ARR for SaaS in India seed rounds, 2024).
- Comparable transactions: benchmark against similar Indian funded startups.

## Due Diligence Checklist (Angel/Seed)
- Founder background and domain expertise.
- Problem validation evidence (customer interviews, surveys).
- MVP and early usage data.
- Cap table and any existing agreements.
- Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM with bottoms-up calculation).
- Competitive landscape.
- Revenue model and unit economics hypothesis.
